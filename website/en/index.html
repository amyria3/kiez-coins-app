Why you should consider switching:

  • What it says on the tin – a pro-social network
  • Chaining you to the app like an addiction – we don’t do that.
  • We don’t tolerate hate speech or content that promotes illnesses like bulimia or profits from them.
  • Your account and content data are stored on servers in the EU – and that’s where they stay.
  • We don’t sell your data and don’t use it to train AI.
  • KiezCoins shows no ads (yet) – we can’t see that far into the future, but for now it stays that way.
  • Your feed is sorted by proximity and time.
  • The idea behind the app: self-organization, connection and synergies – we don’t foster a parallel reality.
  • You decide: use the app for yourself, in groups, in your neighborhood, nationwide – or all of it.

Convinced? Or at least curious?

KiezCoins is in beta. Android downloads the app directly, the iPhone uses the web app.

Android

On Android, you download KiezCoins as an app right here, without Google Play.

  1. Tap “Download the app”.
  2. Open the file kiez-coins.apk when the download is finished.
  3. Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it and tap “Install”.
  4. To update, download the new file here and install it the same way. Your data stays.

iPhone

On the iPhone, KiezCoins runs as a web app in Safari. From the home screen it starts like an app.

Open KiezCoins
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap “Open KiezCoins”.
  2. Tap the share icon in the bottom bar (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen” and tap “Add”.
  4. From now on, start KiezCoins from the new icon and sign in once.

Safari asks for camera, photos and location the first time. The web app has no push notifications. Updates arrive automatically on the next start.

In the browser

No installation, right in the browser. Try the app without an account or create one – whichever you prefer.
